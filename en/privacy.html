Privacy and advertising

Privacy notice

This page explains third-party services, advertising consent choices and their effects on the Aeterna website. Last updated: 2026-08-02.

Hosting and downloads

This static site is served through GitHub Pages. Download links take you to GitHub Releases; GitHub and its services handle those requests under their own privacy policy. No account is required to browse this site or download Aeterna releases.

Your advertising choice

The site does not load the Google AdSense script until you choose “Accept and load ads.” The choice is stored in browser local storage to remember your preference. Declining does not affect page content, GitHub links or downloads. Use “Privacy and ad settings” in the footer to reopen the choice.

Google AdSense

After consent, Google AdSense may request and process device identifiers, IP address, browser information and usage data to serve and measure advertising. Its processing is governed by Google’s privacy policy, your region and advertising settings. Confirm the required CMP, Consent Mode or IAB TCF settings for applicable regions in the publisher account.

Local storage

This site uses only the local storage key aeterna-ad-consent-v1 to retain advertising consent. Clearing site data removes the choice and may show the consent prompt again.

Third-party links

GitHub, Google and other external websites are not controlled by this notice. Read their terms and privacy policies before visiting, downloading or consenting to advertising.

Contact

For questions about site content, downloads or this privacy notice, open a report through GitHub Issues.

You can change your advertising choice at any time.
